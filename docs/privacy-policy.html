Tic-Tac-Toy by Kunal

Privacy Policy · Effective 3 November 2026 (version 1.1, with online play)

In short: Tic-Tac-Toy never asks for a real name, email, phone number, photo or location. It has no ads and no chat. Online play only uses a made-up game name (like "Blue Tiger 27"), a secret 4-number PIN and the games you play.

Playing on your own device

Playing against the computer or with 2 players on one phone works without the internet. The app remembers your game settings (game mode, difficulty level, sound and vibration on/off) and the score board only inside the app on your device.

Online play (optional)

If you choose Play Online, the app talks to our game server. The server stores only:

  • A made-up game name: a colour, an animal and a number (for example "Blue Tiger 27"). Players pick from fixed pictures; nobody types a real name. Guests get a random game name.
  • A secret 4-number PIN (only for players who make a player, not guests). It is stored scrambled (hashed), so nobody can read it, not even us.
  • A sign-in code for your device, so you stay signed in.
  • Your games: moves, wins, losses and draws, invite codes, and the emoji reactions you tap (from a fixed list of 5 emoji - there is no typing or chat).

Other players see only your game name, your animal picture, your moves and your emoji. Like every website, the server's hosting company may keep short technical logs (such as IP addresses) to keep the server running safely; we do not use them to identify anyone.

We do not sell or share this information with anyone, and it is sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection.

How long it is kept

  • Finished games are deleted after 1 day.
  • Guest players who do not play for 90 days are deleted automatically.
  • Your player is kept until you delete it.

Deleting your player

In the app: Play Online → 🗑 Delete my player → Yes, delete. Your player, PIN and wins are deleted straight away. You can also email us (address below) with your game name to ask for deletion. More details: Delete your player.

Ads and third parties

  • There are no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no in-app purchases.
  • No third-party code or services (SDKs) that collect data are included.

Permissions

  • Internet - only used for online play.
  • Vibrate - so the phone can buzz when you play. You can turn vibration off in the app's menu.

Children

The app is made to be safe for children. It does not collect real names, email addresses, phone numbers, photos, location or contacts from anyone. There is no chat: players can only send one of 5 fixed emoji. A parent can delete a child's player at any time as described above.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
