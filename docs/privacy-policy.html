Tic-Tac-Toy by Kunal

Privacy Policy · Effective 2 November 2026

In short: Tic-Tac-Toy does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal information. It has no ads and does not use the internet.

Information we collect

None. The app does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information, and it has no sign-in.

Information stored on your device

The app remembers your game settings (game mode, difficulty level, sound and vibration on/off) and the score board. This is saved only inside the app on your own device. It is never sent anywhere, and it is deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

Internet, ads and third parties

  • The app does not request internet access and makes no network connections.
  • There are no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no in-app purchases.
  • No third-party code or services (SDKs) that collect data are included.

Permissions

The only permission the app uses is Vibrate, so the phone can buzz when you play. You can turn vibration off in the app's menu.

Children

The app is suitable for children. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
