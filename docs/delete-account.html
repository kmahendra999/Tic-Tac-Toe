Delete your online player

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Tic-Tac-Toy and choose Play Online, then tap START.
  2. Tap 🗑 Delete my player at the bottom.
  3. Tap Yes, delete.

Your player is deleted straight away.

By email

If you cannot open the app, email k.mahendra999@hotmail.com with your game name (for example "Blue Tiger 27"). We delete it within 7 days and reply when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your game name, animal and colour
  • Your secret PIN
  • Your wins, losses and draws
  • Your sign-in on every device

Finished games are removed from the server within 1 day. Nothing is kept after that.
